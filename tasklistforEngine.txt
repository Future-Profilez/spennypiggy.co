php artisan risk:monitor-platform

1. Banner ON karne ke liye: 
   POST /api/test/risk/on hit karein.
   - Response: "Risk flags ACTIVATED"
   - Result: Ab Creator Dashboard refresh karein. 
   Aapko top par 3 banners dikhne chahiye:
   - Yellow: Reserve Applied (10%)
   - Blue: Payout Delayed (7 Days)
   - Info: Growth Pacing Active

2. Platform Freeze Test: 
   POST /api/test/platform/freeze
   Result: Ab "Onboarding Paused" ka 
   RED banner bhi dikhna chahiye.

3. Reset (Banner OFF) karne ke liye: 
   POST /api/test/risk/off POST /api/test/platform/normal
   Result: Saare banners gayab ho jayenge.
